"Zero Here" sets the current angle as 0° — lay it against one face of a joint, zero it, then place it against the other face. The reading is how far off square you are.
Works like a standard digital angle gauge — good for bevel setups, squareness checks, and copying an existing angle. Accuracy is limited by your phone's own sensor, typically ±0.3–0.5°.
