EMF Proximity Indicator

Novelty magnetic-field reading only
This is not a voltage tester It reads fluctuations in your phone's magnetometer — the same sensor used for a compass — as a rough novelty indicator only. It cannot tell you whether a wire is live or dead, and it is not rated, calibrated, or certified for electrical safety work. Never use it to decide whether a circuit is safe to touch. Always de-energize circuits at the panel, verify with a properly rated non-contact voltage tester, and follow lockout/tagout procedure before working on any wiring.
This needs access to your phone's magnetometer. Tap below once to allow it.
Your browser doesn't expose raw magnetometer data (mainly missing on iOS Safari and many desktop browsers). This tool needs it and can't fall back to anything else.
