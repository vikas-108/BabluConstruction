Your browser doesn't expose a light sensor. Ambient light sensor access has been removed from most modern browsers (including Safari and current Chrome) over privacy concerns, so this mostly won't work anywhere anymore — it's included in case your browser is one of the few that still supports it.
Even where supported, phone light sensors aren't calibrated instruments — treat readings as a rough comparison between spots, not an exact lux measurement you'd cite in a lighting report.
