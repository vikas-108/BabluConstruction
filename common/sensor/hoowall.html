Knock Test

Compare two knocks side by side
⚠ Experimental — visual aid only, not a hollow/solid detector
This tool listens through your microphone — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
A — Reference knock (somewhere known, e.g. a stud)
B — Test knock (the spot you're checking)
Generally: a shorter, higher-pitched, quickly-fading trace often means a hollow cavity behind that spot; a longer, lower, more sustained trace often means something solid — but this is a rule of thumb, not a measurement. Compare the shapes visually; don't treat either trace as a verdict.
A real stud finder uses capacitance or radar sensing your phone doesn't have. This just lets you see and compare the sound of two knocks — the interpretation is still yours, the same as listening by ear.
