How to use this screen
  • Pick the date at the top, then tap a worker's chip on the right to cycle Present → Half → Absent → blank.
  • Use "Mark all present" to fill in the whole roster for that date in one tap.
  • Tap a worker's name to open their card — set daily wage, add a work note, record advances/payments, and see their site check-ins.
  • Tap + (bottom right) to add a new worker to the roster.
  • Open Geo attendance for GPS-verified check-ins, or Payout summary to see what's owed across a date range.

How to use this screen
  • Tap + next to Site/project to add a work site — name it, then tap "Use my current location" while standing there, or enter its lat/lng manually.
  • Set the site's geofence radius in metres — how far a worker can be from that point and still count as "on site".
  • Pick the Worker checking in and the Site they're at.
  • Tap Check in now and allow location access when your browser asks.
  • Green marker = inside the geofence, verified. Amber/red = far from site — flagged for review.
  • Every check-in is saved under that worker's profile too, so you can review their history from their detail card.
